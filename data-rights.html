Last updated: August 30, 2026

Your Data Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information processed by JcMine.

1. Right to Access

You may request information about the personal data associated with your JcMine account.

2. Right to Correction

You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

3. Right to Deletion

Where applicable, you may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal or operational requirements that may require certain information to be retained.

4. Right to Restriction

Where applicable, you may request that processing of certain personal information be restricted.

5. Right to Data Portability

Where applicable, you may request your personal information in a structured and commonly used format.

6. Right to Object

Where applicable, you may have the right to object to certain types of processing of your personal information.

7. How to Make a Request

To make a data rights request, contact the JcMine team through the available contact channels.

For security purposes, we may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

8. Response Time

We aim to respond to valid requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

9. Important Information

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, maintain security, or fulfill legitimate operational requirements.
